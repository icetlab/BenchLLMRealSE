I will improve performance by eliminating the second pass over all players and instead check the winning condition immediately after each pick. This reduces the overall complexity from O(N + M) to O(M) and avoids iterating over all players when there are few picks. The code also uses `computeIfAbsent` and `merge` for conciseness and efficiency.
