The original triple-nested loop is O(n³). I replaced it with an O(n log n) prefix-sum + sliding-window approach: for each subarray end, maintain all valid starting prefixes in a `TreeMap`, then use `lowerKey` to find the largest prefix smaller than the current prefix, giving the smallest positive sum ending at that index.
