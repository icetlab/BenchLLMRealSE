Looking at the test failures, my sliding window approach has a bug. The issue is that when I shrink `minLeft` to remove extra vowels, I'm modifying the `vowelCount` array which corrupts the state for future iterations. I need to track the minimum left position differently without modifying the vowel counts.

The correct approach is to track the position of the first consonant in the window, and count all valid starting positions between that consonant and the current left boundary. Let me fix this:
