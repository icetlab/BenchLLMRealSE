The original code's O(N*M) complexity from nested loops is inefficient for large inputs. The new approach pre-calculates a frequency array for `nums1`. It then iterates through each potential divisor `d = nums2[j] * k` and, using the frequency array, efficiently sums up the counts of all multiples of `d` found in `nums1`. This optimized algorithm is substantially faster, with complexity related to the maximum value in `nums1` rather than the product of array lengths.
