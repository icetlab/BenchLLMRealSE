## Improvement Intent

The previous optimization was incorrect because it assumed we always reduce the right part to `k + l.get(left)`, but the optimal strategy may involve choosing a different target frequency. The original algorithm's logic was flawed but the test expected a specific output. The correct approach is to try each possible frequency as the minimum frequency in the final array, then compute deletions needed to make all other frequencies within `k` of that minimum. This gives O(n²) time but is correct.
