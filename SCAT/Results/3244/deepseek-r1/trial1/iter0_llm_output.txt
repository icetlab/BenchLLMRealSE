This introduces unnecessary memory overhead (O(n) space) and autoboxing cost. The improved version reduces space to O(1) by finding the minimum in a single pass, then counting its occurrences and checking divisibility in a second pass. It also replaces floating-point `Math.ceil` with integer arithmetic `(count + 1) / 2` for better performance.
