The main performance bottleneck is the nested loop that checks each occurrence of pattern `a` against all occurrences of pattern `b`. This is O(n*m) where n and m are the respective occurrence counts. By using a two‑pointer technique on the two sorted lists, we can reduce this to O(n+m) and still correctly identify all indices that have a matching `b` occurrence within distance `k`.
