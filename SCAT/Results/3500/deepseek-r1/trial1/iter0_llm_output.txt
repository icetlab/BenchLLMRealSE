The main overhead was converting `int[]` inputs into boxed `Integer[]` arrays and sorting them with a comparator. I replaced that with cloning to primitive `int[]` arrays, using Java’s optimized primitive sort, then reversing to get descending order. This preserves the original greedy logic and avoids boxing overhead.
