My improvement replaces this entire process with direct integer arithmetic. By using division and modulo operations, I extract the digits at each position (thousands, hundreds, tens, and ones) from the input numbers. After finding the minimum digit for each position, the final key is constructed arithmetically. This eliminates all string-related overhead and object creation, resulting in a significantly faster and more memory-efficient solution.
