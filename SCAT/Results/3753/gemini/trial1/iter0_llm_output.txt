The original code calculates the maximum difference between an odd character frequency and an even character frequency by using a nested loop. This approach compares every pair of character frequencies, leading to a time complexity of O(U^2) for this part of the logic, where U is the number of unique characters.

The performance can be significantly improved by recognizing that to maximize the difference `odd_freq - even_freq`, we only need the maximum possible odd frequency and the minimum possible even frequency.

The proposed change replaces the O(U^2) nested loop with a single pass over the frequency map's values. This pass identifies the `maxOdd` frequency and `minEven` frequency in O(U) time. The final result is then a simple subtraction. This optimization reduces the overall time complexity without changing the logic or the final result.
