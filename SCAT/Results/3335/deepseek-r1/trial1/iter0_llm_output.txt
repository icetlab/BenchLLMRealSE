The main optimization is to replace the six full-grid scans with a single scan that counts cell values, plus a short walk over the Y-shaped cells. The cost of each Y/other color pair is then computed in O(1), keeping the overall time at O(n²) instead of O(6n²) with repeated HashSet lookups.
