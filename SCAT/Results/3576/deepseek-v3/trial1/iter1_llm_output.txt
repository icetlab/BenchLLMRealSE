Looking at the error, the issue is that when using `ArrayList` instead of `HashSet`, the `remove(node)` call in the DFS method is being interpreted as removing by index rather than by value. When `node` is -1 (for the root's parent), it tries to remove at index -1, causing the `ArrayIndexOutOfBoundsException`.

The fix is to use `remove(Integer.valueOf(node))` to remove by value, and also handle the case where `parent` is -1 (root node) to avoid the removal operation entirely.
