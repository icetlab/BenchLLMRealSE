The original code uses a sliding window with a `HashMap` to count alternating groups. This approach is overly complex and inefficient for a fixed-size window of three. The `HashMap` operations (insertion, lookup, deletion) introduce unnecessary overhead.

My improvement replaces this complex logic with a simple, direct approach. I iterate through the array once, and for each position `i`, I check the three consecutive elements `colors[i]`, `colors[(i+1)%n]`, and `colors[(i+2)%n]` (handling wrap-around with the modulo operator). The condition for an alternating group is checked directly. This eliminates the `HashMap` and the complex window management, resulting in a solution that is significantly faster, more memory-efficient, and much easier to read and understand.
