The original 3D DP tracked the selected-element count only to multiply by `2^(n-ele)`. I replaced it with a 2D subset-sum DP: each chosen element contributes a factor of `1/2`, and the final result is multiplied by `2^n`. This reduces time to `O(n*k)` and memory to `O(k)`.
