The original brute-force scan is replaced with a direct bit-manipulation formula. For an odd `x`, the smallest valid `j` is `x - 2^(c-1)`, where `c` is the number of trailing 1 bits in `x` (obtained via trailing zeros of `x + 1`). Even values have no answer because `j | (j+1)` is always odd.
