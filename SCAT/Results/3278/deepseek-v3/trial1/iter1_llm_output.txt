## Improvement Intent

The previous optimization was incorrect because it only considered pairs where the upper-left point comes before the lower-right point in the sorted order, but the original algorithm considers both orderings. The original problem requires counting pairs where one point is the upper-left corner and another is the lower-right corner of a rectangle with no other points inside. The correct approach is to sort by x ascending, then y descending, and for each point as potential upper-left, find lower-right points that have no other points with y between them.
