Looking at the code, I notice the main performance issue is in the `calculateReverse` and `calculateStraight` methods. They use while loops that iterate through the alphabet one character at a time, which is inefficient. Since we're working with a fixed alphabet of 26 letters, we can precompute prefix sums to get O(1) lookups instead of O(26) per character.

The improvement: Precompute prefix sums for both directions (nextCost and previousCost) so we can calculate the cost between any two letters in O(1) time instead of O(26).
