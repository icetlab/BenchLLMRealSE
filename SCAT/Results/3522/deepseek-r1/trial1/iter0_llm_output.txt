The original implementation repeatedly copies and sorts each \(k\)-sized subarray, making it \(O(n \cdot k \log k)\). I replaced it with a single left-to-right scan that tracks the current streak of consecutive \(+1` differences, reducing the complexity to \(O(n)\) time and \(O(1)\) extra space.
