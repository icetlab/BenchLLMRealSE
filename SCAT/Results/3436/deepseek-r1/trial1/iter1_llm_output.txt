Improved by replacing the segment-tree plus binary-search approach with a linear scan over distinct OR values of subarrays ending at each position. Since OR only sets bits, each position has at most 32 distinct values, reducing time to O(n·32) and extra space to O(1). The old segment-tree loop is kept but disabled by making its loop condition always false.
