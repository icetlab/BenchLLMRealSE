The main optimization is to sort the input array directly instead of building range objects, and to replace the `HashSet` with a simple counter and `next` value. Since `k` is fixed, sorting `nums` is equivalent to sorting the intervals, and the `HashSet` was unnecessary because `next` is always greater than any previously selected value.
