The original solution repeatedly binary-searches occurrence lists for every index. I replaced that with a linear-time monotonic-stack approach: maintain the nearest previous greater element on the fly, and for each value keep only the occurrence indices that are still valid (i.e. not blocked by a greater element). Each index is pushed and popped at most once, so the total time is O(n).
