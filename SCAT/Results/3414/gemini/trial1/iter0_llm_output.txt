2.  The crucial pruning condition `if(move.currentStair - k > 1)` may fail to trigger, as an overflowed negative `currentStair` would not be greater than `k + 1`.
3.  Failure to prune leads to exploring an unnecessarily large number of states, causing a significant performance degradation, potentially resulting in a `StackOverflowError` or a "Time Limit Exceeded" verdict.
