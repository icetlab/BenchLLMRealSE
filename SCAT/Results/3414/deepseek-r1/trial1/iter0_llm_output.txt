The original solution uses recursion with memoization, exploring a state space that can grow linearly with k. The key insight is that the number of ways to reach stair k depends only on the number of up moves U and down moves D, where the final stair is given by \(2^U - D\). With constraints that no two downs are consecutive and the first move can be up or down, the total number of valid sequences can be computed in \(O(\log k)\) time using binomial coefficients. This avoids combinatorial explosion and works for large k.
