The original code's performance is limited by linear-time insertions into sorted `ArrayList`s within a loop, resulting in an O(N^2) time complexity. My improvement replaces this with a solution based on Fenwick Trees (also known as Binary Indexed Trees) and coordinate compression. This allows for efficiently counting elements greater than a value in logarithmic time. By using Fenwick Trees to manage the two subarrays, both querying for the counts and updating them become O(log K) operations, where K is the number of unique elements. This reduces the overall time complexity to O(N log K), a significant performance gain.
